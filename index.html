Decisions and bookings that can't wait

  1. Lake Garda hotel (17–19 Oct) is still open. Book the south-west corner — Salò, Desenzano, Sirmione or Padenghe. From there it's ~2h to Malpensa; from Limone or Riva it's 3h15–3h45, which would wreck your departure day. Shortlist below.
  2. Tre Cime di Lavaredo now requires a pre-booked online slot. €40 per car at pass.auronzo.info, licence plate required, ticket valid 12 hours. You cannot just drive up any more. Book once the forecast firms up (~4 days out).
  3. Cortina has almost no lifts in your window. Faloria closed 20 Sep, Tofana 29 Sep, Cinque Torri chairlift closes 11 Oct — one day before you arrive. Only the Lagazuoi cable car still runs (to 18 Oct). The Cortina half is a walk-from-the-car trip, and most rifugi are shut — carry lunch and water every day.
  4. Val Gardena is the opposite — lifts are good. Seceda, Col Raiser and both Alpe di Siusi cableways run to 1–2 November. Ciampinoi, Resciesa and Mont de Sëura die on 11 Oct. Dantercëpies is already shut.
  5. Törggelen season starts in October. South Tyrol's harvest ritual — a long farm lunch with the year's brand-new cloudy wine. This is the food highlight of the trip and it's worth an hour's drive. Book by phone; some farms don't open until mid-month.
  6. Venice access fee does NOT apply. The 2026 trial ended 27 July. No registration, no QR code, no €5. Ignore every blog that says otherwise.
  7. Rental car: ask in writing for M+S / all-season tyres (not legally required on your dates but mid-October snow at 1,800 m is normal), and confirm the return terminal matches your departure terminal at MXP. That mismatch is the single most likely thing to go wrong on the last day.
One thing to confirm: the exact address of The Oswald Grand Hotel. I've planned it as Selva di Val Gardena (Wolkenstein). Parts of Str. Mëisules in Selva are inside a restricted-traffic zone — email the hotel for the exact driving approach and whether they need your plate registered.
