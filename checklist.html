Book now · Call in late September

Now (two months out):

  • Lake Garda hotel, 17–19 OctSalò, Desenzano, Sirmione or Padenghe. The only unbooked night of the trip.
  • Venice hotel, 16–17 OctDorsoduro or Cannaregio, on the island.
  • Rental carRequest M+S / all-season tyres in writing. Confirm the return terminal matches your departure terminal.
  • Antiche Carampane, VeniceBooks out weeks ahead. Friday 16 Oct.
  • Villa del Balbianello interior tourticket.fondoambiente.it, or +39 0344 56110. Tuesday 6 Oct.
  • Törggelen farm, Sat 10 OctVillandro (Oberpartegger / Winklerhof / Pschnickerhof) or Fronthof at Aica di Fiè. Confirm they're serving that early in the month.
  • El Brite de Larieto+39 368 7008083, for Mon 12 Oct. And SanBrite (+39 0436 863882) if you want the star.
  • Zenato tasting, Mon 19 OctMon–Sat only. Classic tour €40.
  • Guerrieri Rizzardi, Sun 18 Octvisite@guerrieri-rizzardi.it — one of the few open on a Sunday.
  • Confirm the Oswald Grand Hotel addressAnd ask about the Selva restricted-traffic zone and plate registration.
  • Zannes / Zans car park, Val di FunesPre-bookable online in 2026 — check whether Sat 10 Oct needs a reservation.
  • Forcella Sassolungo "coffin" gondola — closing dateSingle-sourced as 11 Oct 2026. If it's right, Sunday morning is your only chance. Verify early.
  • Install EasyPark or MooneyGoBlue-line parking across Lombardy and the Dolomites, without hunting for coins.

  • Lake Como autumn ferry timetableThe summer one expires 4 Oct and the replacement wasn't published. navigazionelaghi.it or 800 551 801.
  • Rifugio Croda da Lago / Palmieri+39 0436 862085 — is it serving lunch mid-October?
  • Rifugio Lagazuoi+39 0436 867303. And confirm the cable car's last day (sources say 18 or 20 Oct).
  • Rifugio Auronzo+39 0435 39002 — open while the toll road is?
  • Villa del Balbianello season endFAI hadn't published a 2026 calendar.
  • Veneto Strade Dolomite pass tyre ordinancevenetostrade.it, the week before you travel.
  • Sorapis trail 215 conditionsAsk the Passo Tre Croci hotel the evening before — they'll know if the ladders are iced.
