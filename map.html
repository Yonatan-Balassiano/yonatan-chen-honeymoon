Getting it into Google My Maps — two minutes

  1. Go to mymaps.google.com and sign in.
  2. Click + Create a new map.
  3. Click Import under the first layer.
  4. Upload Honeymoon-Italy-2026-map.kml.
  5. The seven folders arrive as seven layers you can tick on and off. Rename the map and share it with each other.

On your phone it appears under Google Maps → Saved → Maps. My Maps is view-only in the mobile app — edit on desktop. If the KML import misbehaves, the zip also has CSVs: import one, choose Latitude and Longitude as the position columns and Name as the title. One CSV import = one layer, so do the seven files in map-csv-layers/ one at a time.

On pin accuracy: the coordinates are approximate — typically within 100–300 m. That's enough to see the shape of each day and navigate to the right area, but it isn't survey-grade, and a few (the Oswald Grand Hotel especially) are placed on the village rather than the building. Every pin description therefore ends with an "Open exact location in Google Maps" link that resolves precisely. Use the pin for planning and the link for driving.
A quick way to use it in the field: switch off every layer except Parking when you arrive somewhere, and except Rain & cold fallbacks when you wake up to bad weather. Those two layers are the ones that answer a question you actually have at the moment you have it.
