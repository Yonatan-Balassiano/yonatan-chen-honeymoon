## The eight things that shape the trip

1. **Lake Garda hotel is the only open decision.** South-west corner — ~2h to MXP. Limone/Riva are 3h15–3h45.
2. **Tre Cime now requires a pre-booked online slot** — pass.auronzo.info, €40/car, plate required, 12h validity.
3. **The "coffin gondola" (Forcella Sassolungo) closes 11 October** — one day before you drive over Passo Sella. Sunday morning or never. *Date single-sourced — verify early.*
4. **Cortina has almost no lifts.** Faloria closed 20 Sep, Tofana 29 Sep, Cinque Torri chairlift 11 Oct. Only **Lagazuoi** runs, to 18 Oct. Most rifugi shut — carry lunch and water daily.
5. **Val Gardena lifts are good.** Seceda, Col Raiser, both Alpe di Siusi cableways to 1–2 Nov. Ciampinoi/Resciesa/Mont de Sëura close **11 Oct**. Dantercëpies and **Fermeda** already shut.
6. **Törggelen season starts in October.** The food highlight. Phone to book; some farms open only mid-month.
7. **Venice access fee does NOT apply.** The 2026 trial ran 3 Apr–26 Jul only.
8. **Rental car:** request M+S/all-season tyres in writing; confirm the return terminal matches the departure terminal at MXP.

## Bad weather — plan B for every day

### The three rules that matter more than any single fallback

1. **Reorder within each block, don't push through.** Four days in Val Gardena, four in Cortina. Give **Seceda**, the **coffin gondola** and **Tre Cime** your clear days; let the low, forested, indoor things absorb the bad ones.
2. **Never pre-buy a view.** Seceda's cable car (€74), the Lagazuoi cable car and the **Tre Cime toll slot (€40)** are all money spent on something cloud can erase. Book Tre Cime only when the forecast is firm, ~3–4 days out.
3. **Rain at 200 m is snow at 2,000 m.** Check the mountain forecast, not the valley one.

## Blog list — reviewed

### Newly added
- **"Coffin gondola" — Forcella Sassolungo, Passo Sella** (2,240 → 2,681 m). Two-person standing cabins that never stop; five minutes to Rifugio Toni Demetz. **Closes 11 October** — you cross the pass on the 12th, one day too late. → **Sun 11 Oct, 09:30**, with Alpe di Siusi pushed to the afternoon. ⚠️ *Date single-sourced.*
- **Passo Giau** (2,236 m). Return over Giau on the SP638 instead of retracing Falzarego — same time, makes the day a loop. Larches rivalling Lago Federa. **Rifugio Fedare open year-round**; pass open through winter. Full-day option: **Giau → Monte Mondeval**, 12.8 km, ~5 h. → **Thu 15 Oct.**
- **Lago di Misurina** (1,754 m). Ten-minute stop on the Tre Cime road; you pass it twice. Col de Varda chairlift closed 20 Sep. → **Tue 13 Oct.**
- **Cheaper Seceda route — corrected.** Santa Cristina → **Col Raiser gondola** (to 1 Nov, cheaper, quieter) → walk up via **Pieralongia**, ~**+400 m, 1h15–1h30** → down past Rifugio Firenze. → **Preferred option, Fri 9 Oct.**

### Needs correcting
- **Both blog routes via the Fermeda chairlift.** **Fermeda runs 20 Jun – 20 Sep 2026** — shut nearly three weeks before you arrive. Neither works as written. Col Raiser is fine; you walk the Fermeda leg. The **Cristauta/Praplan** start becomes **+900–1,000 m** with no shortcut.
- **Molveno — skip this trip.** It's in the **Brenta**, a separate massif west of Trento; no day costs less than four hours of driving. From the *north* end of Garda it'd be easy, but you need the south-west shore for Malpensa.

### Fallbacks / optional
- **Segra Sacun market, Ortisei** — Mon 12 Oct, 08:00–16:00. Clashes with the 08:00 Great Dolomites Road start. Take it if you want a slow last valley morning, or if it rains.
- **Geisler Alm / Rifugio delle Odle** — pushing back on the "no go": it's directly on the Adolf Munkel Weg so you pass its door regardless, and the busy/commercial complaint is a high-summer problem. **The real risk is it's shut** — October season unverified.

## Day by day

### Mon 5 Oct — arrive
Land 19:00 → on the road ~21:00. **Eat at MXP T1 landside before driving** — kitchens take last orders 21:30–22:00 and Monday is the standard closing day in Brianza. A8 → Tangenziale Nord → SS36 → exit Nibionno, ~1h15. Arrive ~22:30. Avoid the A36 Pedemontana (plate-based tolling). *Parking free at the hotel.*

### Tue 6 Oct — west Lake Como
**Order matters:** Balbianello closed Mon *and Wed*; Villa Monastero closed Tue.
- Villa del Balbianello, Lenno (~1h10). Gardens €13–14, + interior tour €24–25. Taxi-boat from Lido di Lenno €6, or 20–25 min path.
- Lunch Lenno/Tremezzo → Villa Carlotta (daily, 10:00–19:00 to 18 Oct, ~€15–17.50).
- Ferry to Bellagio as foot passengers (central-lake day pass ~€17.50). Villa Melzi gardens €10.
- Private sunset boat 17:30–18:30 (~€150–200/h) — best single splurge on this leg.
- ⚠️ Autumn ferry timetable not yet published — check navigazionelaghi.it.

**Parking:** park once, use the **C10 shore bus**. **Lido di Lenno** lot (~€1.50–2/h) — satnav to "Lido di Lenno", not the villa. Free: white lines uphill in Lenno/Mezzegra. Villa Carlotta: C10 from Lenno, or Tremezzo lakefront / Cadenabbia pier + 10 min. **Don't put the car on the ferry.**

### Wed 7 Oct — Varenna
- **Sentiero del Viandante, Varenna → Bellano.** ~3 h, ~530 m. Best walk of this leg.
- Orrido di Bellano (€8), lunch in Bellano, train back (~€2–3).
- Villa Monastero (garden + museum €15, last entry 17:30).
- Dinner: Il Cavatappi or Vecchia Varenna.

**Parking — the trick that makes today work.** Park at the **Varenna-Esino-Perledo railway station**: free or near-free, 10 min down, **and it's where your train from Bellano returns**. Fallbacks: SP72 or Olivedo pier lots (~€2/h); free white lines towards **Perledo**. **Never drive into Varenna's centre.**

### Thu 8 Oct — to Val Gardena via Bergamo
~340 km, 4h–4h45, toll ~€21–25. Optional Monte Barro walk first. **Break in Bergamo Città Alta** — funicular up (~€3 return), casoncelli for lunch. Then A4 → A22 → exit Chiusa → SS242. Arrive ~17:00. Dinner at **Nives** in Selva (open year-round).

**Parking:** Monte Barro free at **Piazzale Cappello degli Alpini** or the **Eremo**. Bergamo free P+R at **Fiera di Bergamo** (300 spaces, line 1) or **Ex Bonini**, Via San Bernardino 149 (line 6). Paid: Ex Gasometro, Via Baschenis, Stadio Atalanta (max 2.20 m). **Never drive to Città Alta.**

### Fri 9 Oct — Seceda ★
**Preferred (cheaper, quieter):** Santa Cristina → **Col Raiser gondola** → ridge via **Pieralongia** (~+400 m, 1h15–1h30) → down past **Rifugio Firenze** for lunch (closes 12 Oct; 11:30–15:00).
**Classic:** Ortisei–Furnes–Seceda cable car, ~€74 return, first up 08:30, park at the station. 8.9 km, +575 m, 3–3.5 h.
⚠️ **Fermeda chairlift is closed** — ignore any blog route using it.
Also open: Baita Sofie, Malga Odles (both to 2 Nov).

### Sat 10 Oct — Val di Funes ★, then Törggelen
**Morning:** ~50 min to **Zannes / Zans**, **Adolf Munkel Weg** — 9.2 km, +378 m, 3–3.5 h, under the Odle north wall. Huts: Dusler Alm, **Geisler Alm** (optional), Gschnagenhardt Alm. Plus **Santa Maddalena** — park in the village, walk 20–30 min; cars barred from the viewpoint May–Nov. **Zannes parking may need an online booking.**

**Afternoon — Törggelen** at Villandro, 15 min from Zannes. **Oberpartegger, Winklerhof, Pschnickerhof**; also Rielingerhof, Patscheiderhof (Renon), Fronthof (Aica di Fiè). Roast chestnuts, barley soup, Schlutzkrapfen, Knödel, Schlachtplatte, Krapfen/Strauben, pine schnapps. Drink Nuier/Siaßer or Valle Isarco Sylvaner/Kerner/Veltliner. ⚠️ **Phone ahead.** Driver doesn't taste.

### Sun 11 Oct — coffin gondola ★, then Alpe di Siusi ★
⚠️ **Most date-critical day of the trip.**
- **09:00** drive to **Passo Sella** (20 min, free parking).
- **09:30 Forcella Sassolungo "coffin" gondola** → Rifugio Toni Demetz, 2,681 m. Down ~11:00.
- **11:30** to **Ortisei**, **Mont Sëuc gondola** (to 2 Nov) up to Alpe di Siusi.
- **Afternoon:** meadow circuit up to 14.4 km, +483 m, 4.5 h — or a short loop. Lunch: Rif. Sassopiatto (to 18 Oct), Rif. Alpe di Tires (to 19 Oct), Malga Schgaguler (to 1 Nov), Rif. Bolzano and Malga Gstatsch (last day).
- Also last day for Ciampinoi, Resciesa, Mont de Sëura. **Don't drive up to Compatsch.**

### Mon 12 Oct — Great Dolomites Road ★ Carezza + Canazei
~150 km, ~4 h driving, no tolls.
- **08:00** over **Passo Sella** (2,244 m).
- **09:30 Lago di Carezza** (1,534 m) — paid lot on the SS241, 20–30 min boardwalk. Early for still water. No swimming, no water's edge.
- **11:00 Canazei** (1,465 m) — coffee and lunch. Sass Pordoi cable car probably closed.
- **12:45 Passo Pordoi** → Arabba → **Passo Falzarego**.
- **~15:20** Cortina → Passo Tre Croci. Dinner **El Brite de Larieto** (+39 368 7008083, closed Wed).

**Fallback:** **Segra Sacun** and/or **Vallunga**, then the short pass route (85 km, 2h45), dropping Carezza and Canazei.
**Snow fallback:** valley route, 145 km, ~2h20.

### Tue 13 Oct — Lago di Braies at dawn ★, then Tre Cime ★
~07:00 to ~17:00, ~3 h driving.
- **07:00** leave for **Lago di Braies** (1,496 m), ~55 km, ~1 h. Sunrise 07:26; arrive ~08:00. Shore loop 3.5 km, ~1 h. **No reservation in October.**
- **09:45** to Tre Cime via Dobbiaco → Landro → Misurina, ~50 min. **Stop at Lago di Misurina** (10 min).
- **Tre Cime:** book the toll slot (€40, 12 h, autumn access ~08:00–18:00) for late morning. Loop 8.8–10 km, +425 m, 3–4 h. **Locatelli closed** — carry everything.
- Add **Cadini di Misurina** (3.9 km, +144 m, 1–1.5 h) — exposed final spur, dry only.
- ⚠️ **10-hour day.** Split option: Tre Cime alone today, **Braies Wednesday morning** before Lago Federa.

### Wed 14 Oct — Lago Federa / Croda da Lago
From Ponte di Ru Curto (free trailhead parking): 8.4 km / +469 m / 3 h out-and-back, or 13.1 km / +700 m / 5 h circuit. **The most famous larch location in the Dolomites.** Rifugio Croda da Lago / Palmieri — call **+39 0436 862085**. El Brite closed Wednesdays.

### Thu 15 Oct — Lagazuoi, Cinque Torri, home over Passo Giau ★
- **Lagazuoi cable car** from Passo Falzarego to 2,752 m — the last lift running (to 18 Oct). Free parking. First up 09:00, last down 17:00. Terrace lunch (+39 0436 867303).
- **Free parking at Col Gallina.** **Cinque Torri / Averau / Nuvolau circuit** — 9.6 km, +600 m, ~3h45. Chairlift and all huts closed; carry food. WWI open-air museum.
- **Return over Passo Giau** (SP638) — same time, makes it a loop. Rifugio Fedare open year-round.

### Flex — Lago di Sorapis
Trailhead at Passo Tre Croci. 11.5 km out-and-back, ~500 m, ~4 h. **Trail 215 has cabled exposed ledges, metal ladders and polished rock, north/east-facing and shaded — iced ladders are a real October risk.** Rifugio Vandelli closed 20 Sep. Mildest, driest day only; mid-morning start, microspikes. Ask the hotel the night before.

### Fri 16 Oct — to Venice
Leave ~10:30. SR48 → SS51 → A27 → Mestre → Ponte della Libertà. ~170 km, 3h–3h30, toll ~€8.20.
- **Park at Tronchetto, €29/24h.** Max height 2.10 m. Don't stay in Mestre to save €14.
- **Two 24h vaporetto passes, €25 each.**
- Zattere / Dorsoduro afternoon. 18:00 Cannaregio bacaro crawl: alla Vedova, Vino Vero, Al Timon. Cash.
- 20:30 **Antiche Carampane** (~€70–90, book weeks ahead, closed Sun–Mon). Or alle Testiere, or Local (★).
- 22:30 **Vaporetto Line 1 down the Grand Canal after dark.**
- October is *moeche* season — order them if you see them.

### Sat 17 Oct — Venice → Lake Garda
- 09:00 Rialto market. 11:00 **All'Arco** (San Polo 436) — closed Sun, shuts ~14:00.
- 13:30 collect car, A4 west. 153 km, 1h45–2h, toll €11.10. Arrive ~16:00.
- ⚠️ **Sirmione:** 24/7 camera ZTL. Email your plate to the hotel 24–48 h ahead.

### Sun 18 Oct — Lake Garda
Sunday: most wineries are Mon–Sat.
- 09:30 Sirmione — Castello Scaligero, **Grotte di Catullo**. Go early.
- 12:30 **Valeggio sul Mincio** — *tortellini di Valeggio*, the "nodo d'amore".
- 15:00 **Guerrieri Rizzardi**, Bardolino — daily 10:00–18:30, from ~€15 (visite@guerrieri-rizzardi.it). Cantina Zeni's museum free.
- Dinner: Esplanade (Desenzano, ★) or Tancredi (Sirmione, ★); simpler — Il Giardino delle Esperidi, Bardolino (closed Tue) or Osteria al Torcol.

### Mon 19 Oct — Franciacorta, then MXP
- 10:00 **Zenato**, Peschiera — Classic tour €40, Mon–Sat, booking essential.
- 13:00 → **Franciacorta** (~40 min). Ca' del Bosco or Bellavista, lunch among the vines.
- 17:00 leave. **19:45–20:00 at the rental return lot.** Check in by ~20:30 for a 23:00 non-Schengen flight.
- ⚠️ Confirm the rental returns at the same terminal you fly from. Split the driving so whoever drives doesn't taste.

## Parking

| Line colour | Means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| White | Free | Just park. Check for a time limit or *disco orario* sign. |
| Blue | Paid | Meter or app. Usually enforced ~08:00–20:00; often free outside — read the sign. |
| Yellow | Residents / disabled | Never. |

- Install **EasyPark** or **MooneyGo**. Many older lakeside meters are coin-only; carry €1 and €2 coins.
- **Set the satnav to the car park, never the destination.**
- **Walk uphill to save money** — lakefront is blue-line paid, white lines one terrace up are free.
- **October helps** — several lakeside lots shorten paid hours after the season ends.
